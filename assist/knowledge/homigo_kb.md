## What is Homigo?
keywords: homigo, about, company, kya hai, kaun, platform, app, website, क्या है, होमिगो, बारे में
audience: all
Homigo is a door-to-door services marketplace. Customers book verified professionals for home repair, cleaning and domestic help, security, health at home, events, daily essentials and more. Every booking is handled by a verified local Area Partner and their team of ID-verified professionals.

## How does booking work?
keywords: book, booking, kaise, process, steps, order, बुकिंग, बुक, kaise kare, book karna, how to book
audience: all
1) Pick a service and see the price upfront. 2) Choose the items and a time slot. 3) Let Homigo send the nearest verified pro, or choose a provider yourself. 4) Share the start OTP when the pro arrives. 5) Pay online or after the service, then rate the job. I can also book it for you right here in chat.

## Choose a provider or nearest available pro
keywords: provider, choose, nearest, partner select, apna, pro choose, chunna, broadcast, specific
audience: customer
On the service page you can choose "Nearest available pro" (we send the job to the closest verified partners and the first to accept gets it) or "Choose a provider" (pick a partner by rating and distance).

## Prices and extra work
keywords: price, rate, cost, charge, kitna, paisa, daam, extra, add-on, material, कीमत, दाम, चार्ज, hidden
audience: all
You see the full price before you book. If the pro finds extra work or needs material, they add it in the app with the price — nothing is added to your bill unless you approve it.

## Payment options
keywords: payment, pay, upi, cash, online, card, bhugtan, paisa kaise, भुगतान, पेमेंट, razorpay
audience: customer
You can pay after the service (cash or UPI to the pro) or pay online at booking. For online payments, open your booking and tap Pay. Never share card or bank details in chat.

## Cancellation and refund
keywords: cancel, cancellation, refund, wapas, paisa wapas, radd, रद्द, रिफंड, cancel fee, reschedule, पैसा वापस, पैसे वापस, वापस
audience: customer
You can cancel from My Bookings. Cancelling well before the slot is free; a small fee may apply close to the visit time. You always see the fee and any refund before you confirm. Online payments are refunded to the original payment method.

## OTP safety
keywords: otp, start otp, code, safety, suraksha, ओटीपी, completion otp
audience: all
The job starts only after you share the start OTP with the pro at your door, and finishes with your completion OTP. Only you can see these OTPs — never share them on a call.

## Verified professionals and safety
keywords: verified, verification, safe, safety, police, id, background, bharosa, भरोसा, सुरक्षित, trust
audience: all
Every Area Partner passes business KYC, bank and document checks before going live. Every professional on their team is ID-verified and approved before their first job. Your exact address is shared only with the partner who accepts your job.

## Service warranty
keywords: warranty, guarantee, redo, not satisfied, problem after, वारंटी, gaaranti
audience: customer
Many services come with a service warranty (shown on the service page). If something isn't right within the warranty period, raise a support ticket from the booking and we'll get it fixed.

## Tools and supplies (kit)
keywords: kit, supplies, tools, saman, material, cleaning material, bring, सामान
audience: all
For some services the pro brings their own tools and supplies. The service page shows if the kit is included or optional, and any kit charge is shown before you book.

## Tips
keywords: tip, tips, bakshish, inaam, टिप
audience: all
After a completed job you can add a tip from the booking screen. 100% of the tip goes to the professional — Homigo takes no commission on tips.

## Reviews and ratings
keywords: review, rating, feedback, stars, रिव्यू, rate
audience: customer
You can rate and review a booking after it is completed (within 7 days). Reviews help other customers and our quality team.

## Offers and coupons
keywords: offer, coupon, discount, promo, code, chhoot, छूट, ऑफर
audience: all
Active offers are on the Offers page. Apply the coupon code at checkout — the discount is shown in the price before you confirm.

## Where is Homigo available?
keywords: city, area, location, available, service area, kahan, shehar, शहर, near me, pincode
audience: all
Homigo shows services from verified partners near your location. Choose your city or use GPS at the top of the site — if a service shows "Not available in your area yet", we're adding partners there soon.

## Contact support
keywords: support, help, contact, complaint, shikayat, शिकायत, problem, issue, customer care, agent, human
audience: all
I can create a support ticket for you right here and share a summary of our chat with the team. Logged-in users can also see and reply to tickets on the Support page.

## How to become a partner
keywords: partner, join, register, become partner, vendor, business, partner kaise bane, पार्टनर, जुड़ें, provider
audience: all
Register with your mobile OTP and choose "Join as partner", then: set up your business profile and services, upload KYC (PAN, masked Aadhaar, business proof, address proof), add your bank account, add your team, and submit for verification (usually 1–3 working days). Once approved, choose a plan and go live.

## Partner documents
keywords: documents, kyc, pan, aadhaar, gst, udyam, cheque, kagaz, कागज़, दस्तावेज़
audience: partner
You need: PAN, Aadhaar (masked copy is fine), business proof (Udyam / shop licence / GST if you have one), address proof, and a cancelled cheque or passbook for payouts. Each coworker needs an ID proof. Aadhaar, PAN and bank details are stored masked.

## Go-live checklist
keywords: go live, live, checklist, pending, kya baaki, approved, activate, लाइव, बाकी
audience: partner
To receive bookings you need: approved verification, an active subscription plan, a verified bank account, a rate card for your services, the minimum number of approved coworkers, and not being on holiday. Ask me "What's pending for go-live?" and I'll check your account.

## Commission and plans
keywords: commission, plan, subscription, fee, charges, kitna katega, कमीशन, प्लान, gst
audience: partner
There is no joining fee. Once approved you choose a monthly plan — higher plans have a lower commission. Commission is charged only on completed bookings, on the service value only; materials, kit charges and tips are not commissioned. GST applies on the commission. You can see plans in your partner panel under Subscription.

## Partner payouts and wallet
keywords: payout, settlement, wallet, earning, kamai, paisa kab, payment kab, कमाई, सेटलमेंट
audience: partner
For online payments your net earning is added to your Homigo wallet and paid to your bank on the settlement cycle. For cash jobs you keep the cash and the commission is adjusted from your wallet. Check Earnings in your panel or ask me for a summary.

## Job requests
keywords: job request, new job, accept, decline, broadcast, kaam, request, नई जॉब
audience: partner
New jobs near you arrive as requests with a timer. Accept the ones you want — the first partner to accept gets the job. Bookings made directly for you must be accepted within the SLA time or they move to another partner.

## Professional (coworker) job flow
keywords: coworker, professional, job flow, on the way, arrived, start job, complete, kaam kaise, worker
audience: coworker
Open today's jobs, tap "On the way" and then "Arrived". Ask the customer for the start OTP to begin. Add any extra work or material with its price — the customer approves it. Finish with the customer's completion OTP and mark cash collected if it was a pay-after-service job.

## Joining as a professional
keywords: worker, job chahiye, naukri, professional join, coworker join, kaam chahiye, नौकरी
audience: all
Professionals join through an Area Partner: ask the partner you work with to add you to their team in Homigo. Once your ID is verified, you can log in with your phone number.

## Privacy
keywords: privacy, data, personal, number, address safe, गोपनीयता
audience: all
Your phone and address are shared only with the partner and professional handling your booking. Chats with Homigo Assist are saved to improve support; please don't share Aadhaar, card or bank numbers here.

## Admin assistant
keywords: admin, dashboard, summary, report, verification queue, staff
audience: admin
For staff I can show today's dashboard numbers, look up a booking by number, and show the verification queue. I only read information — approvals and changes are done in the admin panel.
